The Team

Founded 1929

Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest surviving and most successful team in Formula 1 history, founded by Enzo Ferrari.

16 World Titles

Ferrari holds the record for the most Constructors' Championships won by any team in F1 history.

Maranello, Italy

Every Ferrari F1 car is designed and built at the legendary factory in Maranello, the heart of the Prancing Horse.

Iconic Ferrari F1 Cars

312T (1975)

Niki Lauda's title-winning car, famous for its transverse gearbox and flat-12 engine.

F2004

Widely regarded as one of the most dominant F1 cars ever, driven by Michael Schumacher.

SF90

The 2019 challenger built to mark Ferrari's 90th anniversary in motorsport.

Legendary Drivers

  • Michael Schumacher — 5 World Championships with Ferrari (2000–2004)
  • Niki Lauda — 2-time World Champion, survivor of the 1976 Nürburgring crash
  • Kimi Räikkönen — 2007 World Champion, fan favorite for his ice-cool radio calls
  • Fernando Alonso — Brought Ferrari agonizingly close to the title in 2010 and 2012
  • Charles Leclerc — Current era Ferrari driver and Monaco's own prodigy
